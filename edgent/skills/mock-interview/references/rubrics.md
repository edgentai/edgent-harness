# Interview rubrics

Rows per format. Each row is scored 0-4 by the critic: 0 absent, 1 attempted but wrong or vague, 2 adequate
with gaps, 3 strong, 4 strong and extended under challenge. Map to learner quality as the same number plus
one, capped at 5.

## Technical

| Row | What earns 3 | What costs points |
|---|---|---|
| Problem understanding | restates the problem, names inputs, outputs and constraints before solving | starts coding without stating what is being computed |
| Correctness | the solution runs and produces the right output on the given and the edge cases | fails an edge case the follow-up named; cannot say why |
| Reasoning | explains why the approach works, in steps | "it just works", or a correct solution with a wrong explanation |
| Complexity and trade-offs | states time and space, names the alternative and why it was rejected | guesses complexity; no alternative considered |
| Under change | adapts the solution to the changed constraint, or says precisely why it cannot | restarts from scratch, or claims it still works when it does not |
| Communication | says what they are doing while they do it; asks when unsure | silence followed by an answer; or narration that hides the thinking |

## Oral exam or viva

| Row | What earns 3 | What costs points |
|---|---|---|
| Precision of claims | states the claim with its conditions and its support (a derivation, a result, a source) | overstates; "always", "never", "obviously" without conditions |
| Depth of why | explains the mechanism one level down | restates the claim in other words |
| Limits | names where the claim stops holding and what happens there | denies limits; or concedes everything under mild challenge |
| Handling challenge | meets a challenge with an argument, or concedes honestly and says what would settle it | changes the subject; argues from authority |
| Command of the material | locates the claim in the chapter, thesis or paper and relates it to its neighbours | isolated facts; cannot say where the claim comes from |

## Behavioural (STAR)

| Row | What earns 3 | What costs points |
|---|---|---|
| Situation | one specific setting with the constraint that made it hard | generic ("we had a tight deadline") |
| Task | what *they* were responsible for, distinct from the team | "we" throughout; the task is the team's |
| Action | the specific first thing they did, then the next, with the decision points | a list of good practices; no decisions |
| Result | a measurable outcome, and what did not go well | "it went well"; no numbers; no failure named |
| Reflection | what they would do differently and why | nothing, or a platitude |

## Teach-back

| Row | What earns 3 | What costs points |
|---|---|---|
| The one sentence | states the idea in a plain sentence a first-year could repeat | starts with notation or a definition |
| The picture | gives one concrete picture and uses it throughout | three analogies; or none |
| The steps | three to six steps in order, each one new thing | skips a step and says "clearly" |
| The naive question | answers the student's confused question correctly at the right step | answers a different question; or gets it wrong |
| Earned abstraction | introduces the term or formula after the instance, pointing each symbol at the picture | formula first; symbols undefined |
